Purple Witch Sprite Parts
=========================

This folder contains the uploaded sprite sheet cut into transparent PNG parts with suggested filenames.
The names are intended to be Codex-friendly for paper-doll assembly and simple 2D animation.

Included categories:
- hair
- face / eyes / eyebrows / mouth / eyelids
- hat
- torso / hips
- arms / leg
- skirt / cloth pieces
- boots
- prop (wand)

Assembly suggestion (back to front):
1. skirt_back_long
2. hair_back_main
3. leg_left_stocking
4. hips_shorts_belt
5. torso_coat
6. arm pieces
7. skirt_front_short + cape_side_left/cape_side_right
8. face plate OR individual face parts
9. hair_front_main + side hair tufts
10. hat_main
11. wand / props

Notes:
- The sheet appears to include both full face plates (face_open_smile, face_closed_o_mouth)
  and swappable facial components (eyes, eyelids, eyebrows, mouths).
- A few names are best-effort guesses for cloth / limb parts based on appearance.
- All files preserve transparency.
